**Ovani royalty-free** (licence PDF in each pack folder): commercial use in games allowed, credit
not required (appreciated as "Sound effects by Ovani Sound"), the raw files must not be
distributed or sold on their own.

**GameDev Market licence** (TomWinandySFX packs, bought on gamedevmarket.net): the licence isn't
saved in the pack folders; GameDev Market's standard licence allows use in commercial games and
forbids redistributing or reselling the raw files. Worth saving a copy of it next to the packs.

Packs deliberately not used: Zapsplat (no licence on file; the free tier requires attribution) and
SAVAGEPCKS (music-production packs, no licence on file).
